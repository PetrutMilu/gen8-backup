Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

gen8-backup is used only by its owner, to back up his own files to his own Google Drive.

It uses Google's drive.file permission. With it, gen8-backup can see and change only the files it has created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive, nor Gmail, Photos or contacts.

Files are encrypted before they are uploaded. No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone, and no other person's data is accessed.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
